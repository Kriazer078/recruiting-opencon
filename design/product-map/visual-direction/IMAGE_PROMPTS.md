## Исходный запрос

```text
Create ONE high quality photorealistic editorial photograph for a modern business recruitment website helping people from Kazakhstan work in hotels in Turkey. Use-case: photorealistic-natural, website demo imagery, not an advertisement with text. Wide landscape framing about 16:9. A contemporary Mediterranean hotel lobby opening onto a bright terrace with a small glimpse of coast in Antalya, elegant but believable stone and wood interior, professional hospitality context rather than a vacation postcard. Two adult hospitality employees, a woman with Central Asian features in her late twenties and a male colleague of similar age, understated realistic staff attire in charcoal and ivory, subtle natural interaction near the reception desk, both approximately full-body, positioned in the right half of the composition. Their faces look candid and natural rather than symmetrical fashion models; no obvious branded uniforms or name badges. Left third is an uncluttered darker stone/wood area that can carry website text later. Daylight, restrained cool-neutral color grading, realistic skin texture, ordinary anatomy and hands, natural working posture, gentle architectural depth, photographic commercial quality, 35mm editorial photography. No text, letters, logos, watermark, badges, floating UI, artificial neon, glossy CGI skin, exaggerated luxury props, or cartoon objects. Keep the image composed as a single believable photo, not a collage.
```

## Направление для реальной фотосъёмки

Сотрудники во время работы: ресепшен, ресторан, кухня, подготовка номеров. Естественная одежда, свет и позы; профессиональная среда. Фото сообщает отрасль и задачу сервиса. Не использовать отпускные кадры как замену изображению работы.

Нужны отдельные горизонтальные и вертикальные кадры с пространством под текст. Перед публикацией реальных материалов получить разрешения правообладателей и изображённых людей. Наличие демонстрационной фотографии не означает, что коммерческие материалы готовы.
